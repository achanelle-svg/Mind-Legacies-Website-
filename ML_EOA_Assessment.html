SECTION 02 OF 13

About
Mind Legacies

A brief reminder of who you are applying to join.

Mind Legacies is a Central Intelligence Holding Company and Parent Organization dedicated to building breakthrough organizations through the disciplined scaling of human intelligence.

This position sits within Head Mind Legacies — not within one of its subsidiaries.

Volunteers may occasionally be assigned to support specific subsidiary projects under the direction of Head Mind Legacies. These assignments remain under Head Mind Legacies governance, and volunteers continue to be Mind Legacies workers throughout.

SECTION 03 OF 13

About the
Position

Understanding the office you would be joining.

The Executive Operations Associate serves within the Office of Executive Administration.

The Office exists to:

  • Coordinate executive operations
  • Maintain organizational clarity
  • Manage executive documentation
  • Oversee reporting systems
  • Enable the effectiveness of the Executive Team

This position focuses on creating order, coordination, and consistency across the organization.

SECTION 05 OF 13

Volunteer &
Stipend Information

Mind Legacies believes transparency is non-negotiable.

Mind Legacies is currently in an early-stage, pre-revenue period. Participation in this office is volunteer-based.

No stipend is provided during the first month. This allows both the volunteer and Head Mind Legacies to determine long-term suitability, and gives the Executive Team time to finalize revenue planning and operational readiness.

Future stipends are intended to come from sustainable organizational revenue, rather than speculation.
