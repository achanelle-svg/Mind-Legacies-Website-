Welcome to the
Gateway

Welcome to the SYNAPSIS Recruitment Gateway. SYNAPSIS is a research institute powered by Mind Legacies.

SYNAPSIS evaluates candidates by first asking whether they are the kind of person this institute is built for — before asking whether they can do the job. The next two sections are SYNAPSIS's Culture & Values Screening, and they are the most important part of this gateway.

Completion of this gateway does not constitute an offer. It is one part of a careful, considered review process, and only shortlisted applicants will be contacted regarding next steps.

SECTION 02 OF 16

Culture &
Values Screening

This is the most important section of the gateway. Each scenario below has two responses. Neither breaks a rule or a policy — both are answers a reasonable person could defend. Only one reflects what SYNAPSIS actually values. Choose what you would genuinely do, not the answer that sounds better.

SECTION 03 OF 16

Culture in
Practice

Answer from real, specific experience — not from principle.

3.1Describe a time you reported a finding, result, or limitation that complicated a story your team would have preferred to tell.
✦ REQUIRED
3.2Describe a time you protected sensitive knowledge or information even when it would have been easier not to.
✦ REQUIRED
3.3Describe a time you changed your mind about something you were fairly confident about, once you saw better evidence.
✦ REQUIRED

SECTION 04 OF 16

Values, in Your
Own Words

No options this time. Answer with your actual gut reaction or genuine belief, in your own words — not the answer you think sounds best.

4.1What's your gut reaction when a colleague points out a real flaw in an idea you're genuinely excited about?
✦ REQUIRED
4.2How do you personally know the difference between a finding you actually believe and a finding you just want to be true?
✦ REQUIRED
4.3If a research direction stopped being commercially exciting but was still scientifically important, how much of your own motivation for it would survive that?
✦ REQUIRED
4.4Someone junior to you delivers a disagreement with you dismissively, maybe a little disrespectfully. What's your instinctive reaction, before you've had time to compose a considered response?
✦ REQUIRED
4.5When you're deciding something today, how far into the future do you actually think about the institutional consequences? Answer honestly, not aspirationally.
✦ REQUIRED
4.6What's your honest, gut-level view on how much a researcher should rely on AI to do the actual thinking, versus supporting their own?
✦ REQUIRED
