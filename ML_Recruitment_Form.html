  • Building governance systems and documentation
  • Developing operational infrastructure

SECTION 03 OF 13

Volunteer Structure
& Stipend Transparency

Mind Legacies believes that transparency is non-negotiable. Please read this section carefully.

Mind Legacies currently operates using a structured founding volunteer model. This means that participation during the institutional foundation stage is volunteer-based.

The first month of participation is an evaluation period during which no volunteer stipend is provided. This period allows both Mind Legacies and the volunteer to determine whether there is long-term alignment in values, capability, and commitment.

Following successful completion of the evaluation period, volunteers who continue with the organization transition into the structured volunteer stipend model according to organizational financial planning and stage-based growth.

Mind Legacies communicates these expectations before recruitment continues because we respect your time and believe informed decisions lead to better outcomes for everyone.

SECTION 05 OF 13

Confidentiality &
Information Governance

How Mind Legacies protects organizational knowledge — and why it matters.

Mind Legacies intentionally protects organizational knowledge. Workers receive access only to the information required for their responsibilities.

  • Different offices possess different authorization levels.
  • Subsidiary information, executive documentation, governance systems, and strategic projects are protected through controlled access.
  • Temporary access is granted only when officially assigned to specific projects.
  • Every successful candidate will sign confidentiality agreements and NDAs before receiving access to organizational resources.

This approach protects both the institution and its people. It is a standard of institutional governance, not a restriction.
