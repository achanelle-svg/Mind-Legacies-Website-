What We Are

The applied studio behind Mind Legacies' products.

MindWorks Technologies exists to translate SYNAPSIS's Structural Predictive Intelligence research into real, usable products, without diluting the science behind them. Where SYNAPSIS asks and answers research questions, MindWorks Technologies builds the tools that put validated intelligence to work for real businesses, brands, and creative teams.

Applied, Not Theoretical

Built for Commercial Use

Governed the Same Way
