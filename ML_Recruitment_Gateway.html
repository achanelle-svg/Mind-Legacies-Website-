Welcome to the
Gateway

Welcome to the Mind Legacies Recruitment Gateway.

Mind Legacies evaluates candidates by first asking whether they are the kind of person this institution is built for — before asking whether they can do the job. The next two sections are Mind Legacies' Culture & Values Screening, and they are the most important part of this gateway.

Completion of this gateway does not constitute an offer. It is one part of a careful, considered review process, and only shortlisted applicants will be contacted regarding next steps.

SECTION 02 OF 16

Culture &
Values Screening

This is the most important section of the gateway. Each scenario below has two responses. Neither breaks a rule or a policy — both are answers a reasonable person could defend. Only one reflects what Mind Legacies actually values. Choose what you would genuinely do, not the answer that sounds better.

SECTION 04 OF 16

Values, in Your
Own Words

No options this time. Answer with your actual gut reaction or genuine belief, in your own words — not the answer you think sounds best.

4.1Two ways to solve a problem exist: one simple and slightly less impressive-looking, one more complex and more impressive-looking — and both would work about equally well. Which would you choose, and why?
✦ REQUIRED
4.2You're capable of doing something in a way that's technically "good enough," and also capable of doing it in a way that's genuinely excellent, at real additional cost to you. What actually decides which one you do?
✦ REQUIRED
4.3Someone junior to you handles a disagreement with you poorly — dismissively, maybe a little disrespectfully. What's your instinctive reaction, before you've had time to compose a considered response?
✦ REQUIRED
4.4If you could only be remembered for one of the following, which would it be, and why: being indispensable to this institution, or having built something here that no longer needs you?
✦ REQUIRED
4.5When you're deciding something today, how far into the future do you actually think about the consequences? Answer honestly, not aspirationally.
✦ REQUIRED
4.6What's your honest, gut-level view on how much a leader should rely on AI to make judgment calls that used to require a person's discernment?
✦ REQUIRED
