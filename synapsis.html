What We Are

Identity-first intelligence, held to a scientific standard.

Structural Predictive Intelligence is the framework SYNAPSIS exists to study: the idea that identity has a structure that can be observed, tested, and used to predict how a person, brand, or organization will actually behave, not just how they say they will. Every product built on this framework is required to trace back to research, not the other way around.

Research First

No commercial product ships ahead of the scientific reasoning that supports it.

Governed Commercialization

All SYNAPSIS commercial activity operates under a published governance and revenue framework.

Long-Term Validation

We are building toward peer-reviewed, clinically validated proof of Structural Predictive Intelligence.
