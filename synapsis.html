What SYNAPSIS Is

Behavioural data tells you what happened. SYNAPSIS tells you who.

SYNAPSIS is an identity intelligence system, and an IP System Venture within the Mind Legacies portfolio — one of three venture categories the parent company builds and governs. Rather than focusing solely on behaviour, it transforms behavioural patterns into structured identity intelligence — insight into motivational patterns, decision-making tendencies, preference structures, and behavioural consistency.

The underlying architecture is proprietary and provisionally patented. Organisations interact with SYNAPSIS through its outputs — structured identity profiles we call Mythoses — not through the mechanics behind them. What matters commercially is what SYNAPSIS reveals, not how it works internally.
