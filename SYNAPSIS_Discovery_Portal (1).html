THE SYNAPSIS SIMULATION

What It Actually Produces

Following your Discovery Session, the customer information gathered is processed through the SYNAPSIS Simulation — our proprietary Structural Predictive Intelligence process. The method itself is proprietary. What it produces is not.

Customer Information

The result is not a survey, and it is not a guess. It is a structured hypothesis about how a defined customer group is likely to think, feel, and behave — often explaining behaviors your team has already noticed, but never fully understood.

This report contains hypotheses generated through the proprietary SYNAPSIS Simulation Framework, intended to support strategic decision-making. It is meant to be used alongside your own organizational expertise, customer research, and executive judgment — not as a substitute for them.

Every report separates what was directly observed in your Discovery Session from what was modeled through the SYNAPSIS Simulation, and from the strategic implications drawn from that model — so you always know where a finding comes from.

THE RESEARCH INITIATIVE

Beyond the Engagement, A Scientific Question

SYNAPSIS is more than a commercial service. It is the applied, real-world testing ground for an active research program: whether Structural Predictive Intelligence is a genuine, distinct category of intelligence — not simply a more detailed way of describing customer behaviour, but a structural approach capable of anticipating it.

That question is not yet answered. Mind Legacies is pursuing it deliberately and in stages, building evidence through real engagements before presenting the work for independent, external scientific investigation. With a client's consent, anonymized findings from an engagement may contribute, in aggregate, to that broader research effort.

We are candid about where that research currently stands: promising, under active development, and not yet independently validated. Every claim on this portal is scoped to what a Discovery Session and the SYNAPSIS Simulation can responsibly deliver today — not to where the underlying research is ultimately headed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

What happens during the Discovery Session?
A structured, one-hour executive conversation covering your organization, its current challenges, your strategic objectives, and how the SYNAPSIS framework may apply. See the agenda above for the full breakdown.
Who should attend?
The sponsoring executive, and where relevant, one or two senior colleagues closest to the challenge being discussed.
Is this software?
No. SYNAPSIS is a Structural Predictive Intelligence framework, applied through an expert-led process. The Discovery Session and the SYNAPSIS Simulation that follows are a service, not a software product you install or manage.
What is Structural Predictive Intelligence?
A distinct approach to intelligence that builds a structural model of a customer group's identity and behaviour, used to anticipate likely patterns rather than simply describe past ones. Applied to your customers, it produces a structured hypothesis about their motivations, tensions, and behavior — not a definitive account of what they think.
Are SYNAPSIS findings scientifically proven?
SYNAPSIS findings are structured hypotheses generated through our proprietary framework, intended to sit alongside your own expertise and research — not certainties, and not a substitute for your own judgment.
Can multiple executives attend?
Yes. Please list all expected attendees in your Discovery Request so we can prepare accordingly.
How long does it take?
The session itself runs approximately one hour, as set out in the agenda above.
What happens after the meeting?
You will receive an Executive Customer Intelligence Report. Where there is genuine fit, you may be invited to discuss the Founding Partner Programme — a four-month engagement, limited to three organizations across the full programme, with one organization admitted per industry sector.
Is payment refundable?
The Discovery Session fee is non-refundable once delivered. See the cancellation and reschedule terms in the Pricing section above for the full policy.
