What V0A is

V0A is the first working version of SYNAPSIS, an identity intelligence system. V0A partnerships are hosted by SYNAPSIS.

It reads behaviour that a company already records, such as purchases, visits, bookings and repeat use, and builds a structured profile of how a group of people behaves. We call that profile a Mythos. It does not need anyone’s personal history, age or biographical details.

The result is a view of the distinct groups inside your customer base, and what appears to drive each one.

What it can do for a company

Most companies know what their customers did. V0A is built to help with why different groups did it.

  • See the separate groups inside one customer base, instead of one blended average.
  • Understand what appears to move each group, so messages, offers and support can fit the people they are meant for.
  • Receive short written reports for leadership on each group, reviewed by a person at SYNAPSIS before they reach you.
  • Check what we find against your own results, with your team.

Data protection and POPIA

These are the questions we expect first. We would rather answer them here than leave them for the meeting.

What information do you need?
Behavioural information your company already holds and may lawfully use for this purpose. We do not need personal history, age or biographical details.
Who decides what is shared?
You do. The scope is agreed with you in writing before any information is shared.
Is anyone profiled without their consent?
Our rule is that SYNAPSIS is not used to model a person, without their consent, for a decision made about or against them. The purpose of each project is agreed with you before it starts.
Who sees the results?
A person at SYNAPSIS reviews every result before it reaches you. Access, storage and retention are agreed with your legal and compliance teams before any information is shared.
What about sensitive information, such as health or financial records?
Where information is sensitive, we agree additional safeguards with your legal team before anything is shared.

How we work with you

Two sides work with each partner.

MindWorks is the commercial side. It looks after the agreement, your day-to-day contact and delivery.

SYNAPSIS is the research institute. It runs the analysis, reviews every result, and studies what each partnership teaches us.

Both sides are involved in what you receive, so the business questions and the research stay connected.

The question we are studying

In each sector we work in, do the behavioural profiles SYNAPSIS builds explain how and why different groups of people act, better than the groupings that sector already uses?

Every partnership adds evidence on that question for its own sector. The sectors we are working in, and the version of the question for each:

Retail and consumer brands
Do behaviour-based groups explain who returns and who drifts away better than existing customer segments?
Health and wellbeing
In digital health, do they explain who stays engaged and who drops off?
Financial services and insurance
Do they explain how different people respond to guidance and to risk?

Other sectors are considered case by case.

Why we are building V0A this way

V0A is the first stage of a staged build. Each stage is tested against real behaviour before the next one begins, because a method like this is only worth trusting once it has been checked against real people.

That is why we build with a few partners who bring real data and real questions, rather than in private. What we find, including what does not work, goes back into the research.

What working together looks like

  1. A first conversation. We agree whether there is a real question worth testing. There is no commitment.
  2. Agreement and scope. The information, the group, the question and the data protection terms, in writing.
  3. The founding partnership, over four months. We build and test profiles on your agreed question, with regular sessions and reviewed reports.
  4. A review in month four, in two separate parts: what the research found, then what continuing would look like. We keep the two apart on purpose.
  5. Your decision. You can stop there or continue.

If you continue after V0A launches

Partners who continue are intended to receive benefits that build over time. The feature built with them stays with them at no further charge, and a preferential continuing rate vests after a year of continuous licensing. The exact terms are set out in the partnership agreement.

In return, partners agree to a defined period of research participation, separate from any commercial decision, so that the evidence from your partnership is kept for the research.

What we do not claim

Results are findings to test, not guarantees.

Findings from a single partnership are reported as supported by the evidence so far, never as confirmed.

V0A does not replace your own analytics team or your judgement. It adds a view of why groups behave as they do.
