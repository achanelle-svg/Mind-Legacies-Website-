Who We Are

A holding company built on tensegrity.

Rather than concentrating strength in a single point, we are structured the way a tensegrity form holds itself upright: through distributed tension and compression across every part, so that no single subsidiary, product, or person carries the whole weight of the structure alone.

"True legacy is not measured by how long a founder remains present, but by how well the organisation continues to thrive in their absence." A. Chanelle Pitso · Founder & President

Mind Legacies was never intended to begin as a company. It began with a simple obsession: how do you build something that truly lasts?

Years ago, I started what I thought would be a single business: a hair care brand. Before launching products or thinking about commercial success, I became fascinated with building systems that could withstand change, uncertainty, growth, and time. I wanted the business to be able to outlast trends, challenges, and even the people leading it.

That curiosity quickly expanded beyond one company. Every new business idea raised the same question: how do you create organisations that are permanent, resilient, and capable of growing beyond the founder? Without realising it, I spent the next two to three years designing system after system, governance, operations, innovation, decision-making, knowledge, brand, until those ideas evolved into more than eighty interconnected intelligence systems spanning multiple disciplines and industries.

The turning point came when I discovered tensegrity, the structural principle of strength through interconnected balance rather than central dependence. It gave language to something I'd already been designing intuitively: an organisation capable of remaining coherent, adaptable, and resilient because every part strengthens the whole.

Only then did I realise I wasn't building independent businesses. I was building the foundation of a parent organisation. That realisation became Mind Legacies, a central intelligence holding company dedicated to designing, governing, and growing enduring organisations, built on the belief that great organisations aren't held together by founders. They're held together by intelligent systems.

About Mind Legacies

How the structure actually works.

This is everything we can share publicly about how Mind Legacies operates as a holding company: how revenue moves between subsidiaries, the values that govern every decision, how tensegrity is applied in practice, how other businesses can join the ecosystem, and where we are ultimately headed.

Royalties & revenue-sharing, in principle

Each subsidiary under Mind Legacies operates its own commercial activity and carries its own True Cost, overheads, and reserve fund. Net profit from commercial revenue is not simply absorbed upward; it is distributed on a fixed, published model that reserves capital for the subsidiary's own continuity, contributes to the shared organizational infrastructure of Mind Legacies, and rewards legacy ownership.

This model is fixed at any given time and can only be changed through formal governance approval, never informally. It exists so that every subsidiary can fund its own future, while still contributing to the shared foundation that makes the whole structure possible.

What governs every decision

  • Intelligence first. No product ships, and no claim is made publicly, before the underlying research or reasoning can support it.
  • Distributed strength. No single person, product, or subsidiary is allowed to become a single point of failure for the whole.
  • Transparency in governance. Financial allocation, reserve funds, and decision authority are documented, reviewed, and auditable, not informal.
  • Legacy over speed. We would rather build something that holds for decades than something that moves fast and collapses.

Tensegrity, applied to a company

A tensegrity structure holds its shape not because one central beam bears all the weight, but because every strut and cable is in constant tension and compression with every other. Remove one part carefully and the structure can often still hold; remove the center of a conventional structure and it collapses.

Mind Legacies is deliberately built the same way. SYNAPSIS validates the intelligence. MindWorks Technologies commercializes it. AfroNovari proves the same underlying philosophy in an entirely different market. Governance documents, reserve funds, and revenue-sharing models exist so that the organization does not depend on any single subsidiary, product, or individual, including its own founder, to remain standing.

An invitation to other businesses

Mind Legacies is not a closed structure. Businesses that share our belief in research-backed intelligence, distributed strength, and long-term legacy over short-term extraction are invited to explore what it would mean to become part of this ecosystem, whether through partnership, licensing, or a more formal structural relationship.

If that sounds like your business, reach out to us directly at Info@MindLegacies.com.

Where this is headed

The long-term goal of Mind Legacies is to build an ecosystem of institutions and businesses that outlasts the founder who started it, that changes how intelligence is studied and applied across South Africa and beyond, and that proves distributed structure can be more durable than centralized dependence, generation after generation.
