About Mind Legacies

A cognitive technology organisation, structured like a legacy institution.

Mind Legacies is a South African intelligence organisation and holding company of intelligent systems and businesses. We research, develop, and commercialise identity-centered behavioural intelligence — starting with our flagship architecture, SYNAPSIS — and we apply the same disciplined, systems-first thinking to every venture under our name.

Rather than relying on any single person to hold institutional knowledge, we build it directly into our systems. The plan moves regardless of personnel — that principle shapes how we design technology and how we run the company itself.

Founder's Story

The architecture starts with a person, so it doesn't have to depend on one.

Amantle Pitso Chief Cognitive Architect & President, Mind Legacies
"Systems outlast people. If the knowledge isn't built into the architecture, it isn't built at all."

Amantle Pitso is the Chief Cognitive Architect and President of Mind Legacies. She is the doctrine and architecture designer behind SYNAPSIS — defining how the system understands identity, continuity, and behavioural intelligence — working alongside a team of technical and operational collaborators to build and deploy it.

Her leadership philosophy is systems-first: institutional knowledge is codified into structures rather than held by individuals, the plan moves forward regardless of personnel, and non-performance is addressed without hesitation. That same discipline runs through every entity built under Mind Legacies, from SYNAPSIS to AfroNovari.
