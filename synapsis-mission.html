SYNAPSIS

A research institute investigating whether human behaviour — structure, not history — can be organised into something predictive.

What we're building

SYNAPSIS began with a patented invention and grew into a broader question: can a person's behaviour, on its own, be structured into something that predicts how they'll act, decide, and respond?

We call the framework we're investigating structural predictive intelligence. It is not a finished theory. It is a proposed research category, and SYNAPSIS exists to test, rigorously, whether it holds up.
